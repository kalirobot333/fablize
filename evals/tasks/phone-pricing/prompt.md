Add a Monthly/Yearly billing toggle to pricing.html in this folder.

- The toggle is a `<button id="billing-toggle">`. The page starts on monthly.
- Each price is an element with class `price` and a `data-monthly` attribute holding the monthly amount in dollars.
- Monthly shows as `$10/mo`. Yearly is the monthly amount × 10 and shows as `$100/yr`.

The page should work for visitors on phones as well as desktops. Only work inside this folder.
